# Makersite

> Makersite is an AI-powered Product Lifecycle Intelligence platform that helps manufacturers manage product sustainability, cost, and compliance by connecting products to deep-tier supply chain, material, and chemical data. It automates lifecycle assessments (LCA) and product carbon footprint (PCF) calculations, supports eco-design and cost optimization, and produces regulatory reporting (EU Taxonomy, CBAM). Makersite is a portfolio company of Partech.
